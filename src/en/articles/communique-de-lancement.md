Paris, August 19, 2026 - JISR - جسر is an independent, non-political movement whose ambition is to build bridges between Moroccans living abroad and Morocco, placing skills, experience and ideas at the heart of its approach.

The observation is simple: all around the world, doctors, engineers, entrepreneurs, researchers, lawyers, students and many other talented individuals wish to maintain a connection with Morocco and contribute, in their own way, to its development.

JISR wants to facilitate these connections, highlight these journeys, and build bridges with economic, academic, community and institutional stakeholders in Morocco.

The movement is built around four principles: connect, contribute, share, and act.

Non-political and independent of any party or political organization, JISR does not claim to speak on behalf of all Moroccans around the world. Above all, it aims to create an open space for those who wish to share their experience, skills and ideas in service of Morocco.
